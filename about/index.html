The question that wouldn't let go

I work in AI and I find it endlessly fascinating. One question keeps coming back: how far can we keep extending the context window before we have to admit that's not really a solution? This series is my attempt to properly understand the problem — and find out if there's something better.

I work in AI. That's the short version.

The longer version is that I spend a lot of time thinking about a specific question that I can't quite shake: how far can we keep extending the context window before we have to admit that's not really a solution? Does an LLM actually need to remember everything you've ever said to it? And if it doesn't — what should it remember, and how should it decide?

These aren't purely academic questions for me. They come up in the real work. And the more I looked at the existing answers, the less satisfied I was with them. This series is me trying to understand the problem properly — from the ground up — and see if there's something better.
